marty-caganize — apply Marty Cagan/SVPG product methodology (INSPIRED/EMPOWERED/TRANSFORMED)

WHAT IT DOES
  Applies the operating model from Marty Cagan's INSPIRED, EMPOWERED,
  and TRANSFORMED to product strategy, vision, team topology, product
  discovery, roadmaps, OKRs, and coaching. Used both diagnostically
  (classifying a described team/artifact against feature-team vs.
  empowered-team distinctions, flagging anti-patterns) and generatively
  (drafting vision docs, strategy docs, OKRs, opportunity solution
  trees, test plans). Fires automatically on product-strategy language
  even without the word "Cagan" — e.g. "our roadmap is just a list of
  features," "how do I coach this PM."

WHAT IT NEEDS
  - Nothing to configure; describe the team, org, or artifact (roadmap,
    PRD, OKRs) you want reviewed or drafted

USAGE
  Just describe the product strategy/team/discovery question or
  artifact — the skill activates automatically. No explicit invocation
  syntax is required.

  /marty-caganize --help   Show this message and exit

FLAGS
  --help    Show this help message without making any changes
